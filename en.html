Kosher products in Japan

Kosher products you can find in Japan, Japanese kosher products you can buy or import from France, and where to get them.

Products in Japan From France

Before you buy

These products are certified kosher only in Japan, if they are made in Japan, or if they carry a kosher symbol. This site lists Japanese kosher products and kosher products you can find in Japan.

⚠ Watch out for KitKat

KitKat are kosher only if they come from the USA or Europe, and it also depends on the factory. Check the country of manufacture and the certification symbol on the packaging, with Is It Kosher or with your rabbi.

Japanese kosher products, to find in France

Japanese products you can buy or import from France.

In Paris, the Japanese district is around the Opéra and Pyramides metro stations. The shop Kioko / 京子 sells most of the products on this list, mainly at Opéra, along with fresh vegetables such as shiitake, enoki, shiso or satsuma-imo.

Some Asian and Japanese products with a kosher certification are also sold at stores such as Hypercasher (for example nori or rice vinegar). Whenever possible, buy a product that carries a kosher stamp.
